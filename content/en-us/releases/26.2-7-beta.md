### Improved

- **Chunks are no longer compressed twice.** Chunk records, player data and data files are already compressed, but
  the encrypted link deflated them again: no smaller (100.1%), just CPU on both ends. They are now sent as they are.
  With one worker pregenerating 2,601 new chunks, **Storia Relay used about 23% less CPU** (18.6 s down to 14.4 s,
  average of two runs each). Other messages are still compressed. The wire format is unchanged.
- **The relay remembers where players were**, also after a restart, so a returning player still joins the worker
  that runs their area (`storia-cluster-positions.txt` in the cluster world).
- Link reports and move confirmations use a small shared pool instead of a new thread each time.
- Website: one GitHub request per build instead of eight.

### Other

- Tested: two workers with bots (moves, merging, balancing, `/stop`), a relay that refuses writes until fixed (no
  write lost), shared data (400 of 400 concurrent increments), positions loaded again after a relay restart.
