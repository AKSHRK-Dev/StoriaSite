### New

- **Standby relay**: a second relay can run as a standby and keep a live copy of everything the active relay stores.
  If the active relay's machine or disk is lost, type `promote` on the standby to take over. [[relay#standby|Standby relay]]
  - **No confirmed write is lost**: while the standby is in sync, workers are answered only once a write is on both relays.
  - A stopped or slow standby never stops the active relay (it goes on alone after 2 s; the standby copies the world again when back).
  - `promote` refuses while the active relay still runs, and when the standby was not in sync (`promote force` overrides).
  - The old relay, started again later, becomes the standby by itself.
  - Workers and Storia Proxy accept a list of relays (`"relay-a:25590,relay-b:25590"`) and connect to the active one.

### Improved

- **Cluster workers load and save chunks on 8 threads** (when `chunk-system.io-threads` is left at -1). Their chunk I/O
  goes over the network, so overlapping round trips is faster: pregenerating 2,601 chunks on one worker went from 88 s to 59 s.

### Fixed

- **A worker keeps its index (its range of entity ids)** after the relay restarts or another relay takes over. Before,
  with two or more workers, reconnecting in a different order could swap indexes and stop a worker from reconnecting.

### Other

- Tested: the active relay killed (kill -9) during a 1,521-chunk pregeneration, then `promote`: every chunk and block from
  before the kill was there. A standby restarted during heavy writes caught up. The old relay came back as the standby
  and both worlds were identical, record for record. Storia Proxy moved to the new relay by itself.
