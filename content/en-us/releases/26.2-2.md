Includes every change since 26.2 (26.2-1-beta, 26.2-2-beta and 26.2-3-beta).

### New

- **Storia Cluster**: several servers run one world, and players move between them without a loading screen. [[cluster]]
- **Tick guard**: in busy places, mobs think less often instead of players being limited. [[tick-guard]]

### Improved

- The **per-player budget** limits only fast movers.
- Storia Cluster is no longer a beta.

### Fixed

- Player data, advancements, statistics and shared data that cannot be read from the relay are **never
  overwritten with empty data**. The player is refused at login instead.
- Messages from the relay are handled in order on one thread, so scoreboard and time changes no longer swap order.
- Relay: reading or writing a region file that had just been closed to make room no longer fails.
- The world settings sent to new workers leave out the relay's own files and files being written.
- Handshakes time out after 10 seconds, so a silent connection cannot hold a relay thread.
- `/storia cluster` asks the relay from a background thread, never from a game thread.
- Storia Proxy: a bad move request no longer ends its connection to the relay.

### Removed

- Terrain-only offload (replaced by the cluster in 26.2-3-beta).
