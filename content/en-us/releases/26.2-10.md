### New

- **Linear region format**: store each region as one Zstandard file (`r.X.Z.linear`) and use about half the disk space.
  Set `region-format.type: linear` in `storia.yml`. Existing worlds are converted as they are used. [[linear]]
- **Standby relay**: a second relay can run as a standby and keep a live copy of everything the active relay stores.
  If the active relay's machine or disk is lost, type `promote` on the standby and it takes over without losing any
  confirmed write. [[relay#standby|Standby relay]]
  - Workers and Storia Proxy accept a list of relays (`"relay-a:25590,relay-b:25590"`).

### Improved

- **Cluster workers load and save chunks on 8 threads**: pregenerating 2,601 chunks on one worker went from 88 s to 59 s.
- **Chunks are no longer compressed twice**: Storia Relay uses about 23% less CPU.
- The relay remembers where players were, also after a restart.

### Fixed

- **No data is lost when the relay cannot store a write** (a full disk, for example). Writes are kept and sent again;
  one still refused after 10 minutes is moved to `cluster-spool/failed/` instead of being deleted.
- Scoreboard changes made while the relay is away wait, in order, instead of being dropped.
- A worker keeps its index (its range of entity ids) after the relay restarts or another relay takes over.
- Time and weather reports no longer pile up, and contraption links respect the hand-over pause.
- Relay: connections that never say who they are are closed after 10 seconds, and the world is written to disk on stop.
- Clearer error messages in the [[plugin-api]].

### Other

- When upgrading a Storia Cluster, update Storia Relay, every worker and Storia Proxy together. No setting has to
  change: the new features stay off until you turn them on.
- Tested: a single server; two workers with bots (moves, merging, balancing, `/stop`); a relay outage and a relay that
  refuses writes (no write lost); a worker that cannot read the world; scoreboard, time, weather and game rules;
  shared data (400 of 400 concurrent increments); the active relay killed during pregeneration and the standby
  promoted (every chunk kept, both worlds identical); Linear files read and written by the Linear tools.
