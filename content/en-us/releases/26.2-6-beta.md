### Fixed

- **No data is lost when the relay cannot store a write.** Writes kept on a worker while the relay was away are sent
  once it is back. If the relay then answered with an error (a full disk, for example), the write used to be thrown
  away. It is now kept and sent again every few seconds. A write still refused after 10 minutes is moved to
  `cluster-spool/failed/` instead of being deleted.
- **Scoreboard changes wait for the relay.** Changes made while the relay was away were dropped, so workers could
  disagree. They now wait, in order, and reach the other workers once the relay is back.
- **Time and weather reports no longer pile up** while the relay is slow: one report at a time instead of a new
  thread every second.
- **Contraption links respect the hand-over pause.** A cell another worker has just let go of is not handed over
  right away, so that worker's last saves are never refused.
- **Relay**: a connection that never says who it is is closed after 10 seconds.
- **Relay**: on stop (console, Ctrl+C or systemd), the world files are written out to disk before the process ends.

### Improved

- Clearer error messages for invalid namespaces and channel names in the [[plugin-api]].
- Website: search highlighting no longer breaks on words such as `amp`, and "today" / "yesterday" count calendar days.

### Other

- Tested: 638 writes kept during a relay outage, a relay that refuses writes until fixed (no write lost), two
  workers with bots (moves, merging, balancing, `/stop`), scoreboard, time and weather, game rules, shared data
  (400 of 400 concurrent increments), duplicate worker names, and a single server.
