### New

- **Tick guard**: when one place is too busy, crowded mobs rethink their next action less often instead of players
  being limited. Movement, gravity and collisions run every tick, and blocks such as redstone are never touched. [[tick-guard]]
  - Test: a spawn with 1,400 mobs went from 50–55 ms to 32–35 ms per tick (TPS 20).

### Improved

- The **per-player budget** now limits only players **moving fast** (elytra and so on). Players who stand, build
  or walk are never limited.
- `/storia region` shows the crowded chunks behind the load (location and mob type).
